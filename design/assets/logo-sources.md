# Logo sources

Brand marks used in the Relay design boards. Files here are unmodified originals. The boards recolour only where a mono variant exists: Slate and Ivory for Anthropic, black and white for ChatGPT, and the OpenAI symbol, which ships without a fill and defaults to black. All marks are trademarks of their owners. Showing a mark identifies the provider or product an account belongs to. It implies no endorsement, partnership or integration readiness.
